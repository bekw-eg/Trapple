# Local Apple Meadow lobby

The approved illustrated lobby is installed in both saved playable scenes:

- `Assets/_Trapple/Scenes/Hub/Hub.unity`
- `Assets/_Trapple/Scenes/Menu/TrappleUI.unity`

Open Hub in Unity **6000.6.0f1** and press **Play**. MainMenu's Play action also
enters Hub. TrappleUI opens the world map over the same updated lobby; close the
map to move around. A/D or arrows move, Space jumps, E interacts and Escape opens
settings. The current local Unity session was used to verify Play and a real
Space keypress.

The updated Windows development build is available locally at
`Builds/Hub/Trapple.exe`. Launch it and click **PLAY**. This executable was built
successfully, launched, and verified to enter the updated Hub from MainMenu.
Build output is ignored by Git; other checkouts should build their own preview
using **Trapple > Hub > Build Windows Preview**.

## Installed artwork

The scene uses a combined golden-hour scenery plate plus the wardrobe and three
portal sprites imported from the concept handoff. The workshop uses the existing
matched `Workshop/Door/house_empty_doorway.png` facade and its nine door overlays;
the painted closed door in the concept atlas is not used at runtime.
The workshop sits on the left, the wardrobe stands beside it and three distinct
destination portals sit on the right. An open movement lane separates the
stations. The initial 16:9 view shows
the sun in the upper-right, with warm rays traveling down-left. Moving right
reveals the complete rightmost portal through the existing camera follow.

Ground, the left framing apple tree, flowers and low foreground bushes reuse
the existing source-based Apple Meadow resources. The scenery, ground and
stations are separate SpriteRenderers. Physics, UI and interaction components
remain scene-owned. The existing nine-frame door overlay and entrance path are
retained and aligned to the new workshop exterior.

The camera uses a wider view and smaller horizontal follow bounds. Side walls
keep the player in the dressed, visible area. Legacy background/decorations and
floating station labels are disabled. The generated art has no baked player or
HUD; the existing live player and HUD render over it.

## Reapply

Use **Trapple > Hub > Apply Apple Meadow Lobby** after saving open scenes and
leaving Play Mode. The installer imports the images, slices the atlas with
custom foot pivots, updates both scenes and saves them through Unity Editor APIs.
It refuses to overwrite an unsaved open scene. Applying it again replaces its
own dressing root rather than adding duplicate scenery.

The source images and complete generation prompts remain in
`Docs/Art/AppleMeadowLobby/`. Runtime PNG copies are byte-identical to that handoff.

## Remaining limits

- Sky, mountains and hills remain combined in the scenery plate. Independent
  parallax layers and separately animated portal interiors/VFX are not supplied.
- The gameplay player retains the existing pixel animation style; the source
  art and movement system have not been redrawn.
- Visual review used a 16:9 Game view. Ultrawide/mobile framing and target-platform
  texture compression have not been reviewed.
- This task adds presentation to existing stations; it does not implement the
  planned forest, factory or lava levels.

The doorway registration correction and actual Unity renders are documented in
`Docs/Validation/AppleMeadowDoorRepair.md`.
