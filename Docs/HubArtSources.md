# Hub artwork provenance

All assets referenced by the Hub are stored in the repository. No external game
assets or third-party asset packs were downloaded.

## Supplied references

- `Assets/_Trapple/Art/Hub/Source/AppleHeroReference.png`: the user's
  **Trapple Apple Hero Sprite Sheet(1).png**, preserved unchanged.
- `Assets/_Trapple/Art/Hub/Source/WorldMechanicsReference.png`: the user's
  **Trapple World Mechanic Assets.png**, preserved unchanged, for visual reference.
- `Assets/_Trapple/Art/Hub/HeroAtlas.png`: a transparent adaptation of the four
  large characters from the supplied hero sheet. Generated extraction can differ
  in individual pixels from the original. It retains their pixel-art designs and
  thematic details; it is not a full production animation sheet.

## Original generated artwork

The built-in `image_gen` tool produced `MeadowBackdrop.png`, `HubProps.png` and
`WorldAtlas.png`. The final HubProps contains only the workshop, wardrobe and
portal. The supplied pixel-art characters take precedence over the unused apple
in an earlier prop draft. Small clouds, hills, grass, leaves, glows and ground are
original procedural support textures generated by `HubArtBuilder.cs`.
